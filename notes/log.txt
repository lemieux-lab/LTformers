corkendall([1,3,4,2,5], [3,1,4,2,5])

Here the "values" being correlated are gene ID numbers. The test asks whether the gene sitting at rank 1 has a larger ID than the gene at rank 2 in both samples, and so on. Gene IDs are arbitrary labels, so the answer only reflects how the genes happen to be numbered. If you renumbered the genes, for example alphabetically or by Ensembl ID, the same two samples would get a different distance. On real data with 978 genes, those comparisons are essentially coin flips, so every pair lands near 0.5.
